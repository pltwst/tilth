    How to play

    1. Plant. Put the plant at the front of the queue on any empty tile.
    2. Harvest. Every fourth item is the sickle. Tap a plant to harvest it and every matching plant that touches it on the same soil.
    3. Turn the soil. Harvested tiles change to the next soil. The edge at the bottom of each field shows that next soil.
    4. Score big. Each plant in a group is worth more than the last: 1, 2, 3, 4… times its value. Bees add +1 to every plant in their group.
    5. Be patient. Sunflowers, birches and redwoods need a few harvests to grow. Puffballs rot away after three.
    6. Keep room. The garden is over when you cannot plant or harvest.
