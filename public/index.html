    How to play

    1. Plant. Put the plant at the front of the queue on any empty tile.
    2. Harvest. After a few plants, the sickle comes. Tap a plant to harvest it and every matching plant that touches it on the same soil.
    3. Turn the soil. Harvested tiles change to the next soil. The edge at the bottom of each field shows that next soil.
    4. Score big. Each plant in a group is worth more than the last: 1, 2, 3, 4… times its value. Bees add +1 to every plant in their group.
    5. Be patient. Sunflowers, birches and redwoods start as sprouts. You cannot harvest a sprout: the dots under it show the harvests it needs to grow. Puffballs rot away after three harvests, and their dots count down too.
    6. Keep room. The garden is over when you cannot plant or harvest.

    Tilth is a fan-made homage to Topsoil (2017) by Nico Prins. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by its maker.
